Welcome to the Camp Monroe admin. This is the single place you manage every operational part of the business: retreat inquiries, confirmed bookings, the dates published on the public site, the public experience listings, team access, and the homepage copy.

## What the dashboard controls

Everything you do here reflects on **monroemaine.com** within seconds.

- **Retreats** — inquiries from people who want to host retreats or privately rent the whole camp, plus the calendar of what's available and what's blocked
- **Experiences** — the public listings for day trips, camping trips, and smaller-group offerings
- **Site Content** — editable copy on the public homepage, nav, and footer
- **Team Members** — who has admin access
- **Waitlist** — general-interest signups

## Signing in

1. Go to **monroemaine.com/admin** (or click "Login" in the top-right of the public site)
2. Enter your admin email and password
3. If you don't have an account, ask Kyle to invite you from the Users page — you'll get an email with a setup link

:::tip
You'll stay logged in across sessions. If you ever need to reset, click **Sign Out** at the bottom of the sidebar.
:::

## The sidebar

Once logged in, the left sidebar shows every section:

- **Dashboard** — top-level summary
- **Experiences** — public trip listings
- **Retreats** — the biggest section; inquiries, seasons, blackouts, calendar
- **Waitlist** — general-interest signups
- **Content** — editable site copy
- **Users** — team members
- **Admin Guide** — this documentation

The sidebar stays visible at all times. Only the main content area scrolls. Your email and the **Sign Out** link are always pinned to the bottom.

## Your typical day

Most mornings:

1. Open **Dashboard** — see how many new inquiries need attention
2. Click into **Retreats → Inbox** — triage anything in "New"
3. Check the **Calendar** to confirm what's on the books
4. Reply to any prospect emails sitting in your inbox

If you're in setup mode (start of a season, new member buyout, etc.) you'll spend more time in Seasons and Blackouts.
