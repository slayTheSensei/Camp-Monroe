Most days start the same way. This is the rhythm.

## The 5-step rhythm

1. **Open the Dashboard.** It shows: New inquiries count, On Hold count, Upcoming Bookings (next 90 days), Active Blackouts count, and a "Recent Retreat Inquiries" panel.
2. **Click into Retreats → Inbox.** Anything in the "New" tab needs an action today.
3. **Open each New inquiry.** Read Contact + Request + Dates tabs.
4. **Take one of three actions:**
   - **Place Hold** — promising fit, you want to lock dates while you finalize
   - **Confirm Booking** — ready to commit (rare as the first move; usually after a hold)
   - **Decline** — not a fit
5. **Check the Calendar** once you're done — eyeball the month, make sure holds and bookings match what you expect.

:::note
Aim to move every inquiry out of "New" within 24 hours. The prospect already got an acknowledgment email saying "we'll respond within 48 hours" — keep that promise.
:::

## What to look for on an inquiry

Open any inquiry and skim the tabs:

**Contact** — name, email, phone, org. Copy buttons let you grab email or phone for a direct reply.

**Request** — their retreat concept (for host inquiries), audience type, group size, support needs, and any additional notes.

**Dates** — the requested range. This tab also shows **conflicts** — overlapping holds, bookings, or blackouts. If conflicts exist, you can't confirm until they're resolved.

**Triage** — where you set Status, Priority (1-5, host only), Assigned owner, and Admin notes (internal only, never seen by prospect).

**Communications** — every email the system sent + any manual notes you've logged.

## Questions to ask yourself

- **Fit:** Does this align with the Camp Monroe brand and mission?
- **Dates:** Are they clear of conflicts? Are they in an active season?
- **Capacity:** Is the group size workable (8-24 for host, whatever they specified for buyout)?
- **Timing:** Is their requested date far enough out that we can actually deliver?

If all green, move to Place Hold (you almost never Confirm straight from New — the hold gives you time to confirm details over email first). If a clear mismatch, Decline.

## If you're unsure

Leave it in Reviewing status and note your thinking in Admin notes. Come back to it after you've talked with Kyle or gathered more info.
