Sometimes you need to pass on a request. The Decline action is how.

## When to decline

- Poor fit with the Camp Monroe mission (mismatched values, wrong kind of event)
- Group size outside our capacity (too large, too small for the setup)
- Requested dates are in a blackout or outside any active season
- Availability is legitimately full and no alternate dates work
- Prospect has gone silent after repeated follow-ups

## How to decline

1. Open the inquiry detail
2. Click **Decline** in the sticky action bar
3. A dialog appears with an editable message. The default is:
   > "Thanks again for reaching out. We're not able to accommodate this request, but appreciate you considering Camp Monroe."
4. Edit the message to fit the specific situation
5. Click **Decline & send email**

## What happens

- Inquiry status → Declined
- Hold timer cleared (if it was on hold)
- Decline email sent to the prospect via Resend
- Communications row logged (`decline_email`)

## Tone guidance

The default message is intentionally short and gracious. Use that baseline for most declines. When you want to add more context:

:::tip Template ideas
- **Dates don't work:** "Unfortunately those dates are already committed. If you have flexibility for a different window, we'd love to hear back."
- **Capacity mismatch:** "Camp Monroe hosts groups of 8-24. A party of your size would be better served by [alternative if you have one]."
- **Wrong fit:** "This kind of event isn't something we're set up to host. Best of luck finding the right venue."
- **Going silent:** "We haven't heard back on logistics — marking this as closed for now. Feel free to re-engage if plans change."
:::

## Avoid in decline messages

- **Specific pricing** — the Retreats Pipeline v1 doesn't include pricing and these exchanges may be forwarded. Keep money talk for direct email after interest is confirmed.
- **Identifying other parties** — don't mention "we already committed to another group" by name
- **Open-ended promises** — if it's not going to happen, say so clearly

## Reopening a declined inquiry

If circumstances change, open the declined inquiry and click **Reopen** in the action bar. Status flips back to Reviewing. No email is sent. You'd follow up with the prospect manually.
