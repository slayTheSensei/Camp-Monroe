A **season** is a broad operating period when Camp Monroe is open. Dates outside any active season are unavailable to the public — the calendar greys them out, and prospects can't request them.

You'll typically touch this once or twice a year, around season boundaries.

## Viewing seasons

From the Retreats dashboard, click **Seasons** in the top-right. You'll see every season you've ever created, active or not.

Each row shows:

- **Label** (e.g. "Main Summer 2026")
- **Date range**
- **Status:** Active or Inactive

## Creating a season

1. Click **+ New Season**
2. Fill in:
   - **Label** — short, human. Used only in admin views, never shown publicly. Examples: "Main Summer 2026", "Winter Pilot 2026-27"
   - **Start date** / **End date**
   - **Active** toggle — on means dates within it are available to prospects on the public calendar
3. Save

:::tip
You can have multiple seasons active simultaneously. A "Main Summer 2026" and a "Winter Pilot 2026-27" can both be on at the same time.
:::

## Editing a season

Click any season in the list. You can change any field. Changes are immediately reflected on the public calendar.

## Toggling a season on and off

Instead of deleting, just untoggle **Active**. This lets you pause a season without losing the record — useful when:

- You want to stop accepting new inquiries while you review operations
- A season has ended and you want to close it out without purging the history
- You're experimenting with different operating windows

## Deleting a season

Available from the edit page, bottom-left. Use sparingly — it's permanent.

:::warning
Deleting a season doesn't affect existing confirmed bookings or inquiries within it. Those stay in the system; they just won't be available for new inquiries unless another active season covers the same dates.
:::

## Common scenarios

### Starting the next year

In spring, create "Main Summer 2027: May 1 – Oct 31" with active = on. Leave the 2026 season alone — it can stay active until its end date passes, at which point its dates are in the past and it's moot.

### Testing a winter pilot

Create "Winter Pilot 2026-27: Dec 15, 2026 – Mar 15, 2027", active = on. Those dates immediately appear on the public calendar alongside the summer season. If bookings go well, keep it. If not, toggle active = off.

### Shortening/extending a season mid-year

Just edit the start or end date. The public calendar updates live. Any pending inquiries for dates that newly fall outside the season stay in the inbox but can't be confirmed until you adjust the season back or change the requested dates.
